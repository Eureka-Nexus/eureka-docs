# Roadmap

1. BSC contracts are deployed; mining and the Genesis Market are active on BNB Smart Chain.
2. Maintain versioned Miner and Mining Server releases, checksums, documentation and reproducible audit evidence.
3. Continue controlled CPU/GPU end-to-end work verification, settlement verification, recovery checks and production deployment verification.
4. Keep the public website aligned with canonical addresses, current release links and live on-chain status.
5. Operate and monitor the mining network, daily cumulative settlements and automatic payouts. The current automatic payout threshold is 5 EKNX of published unpaid entitlement, checked every 4 hours.
6. Monitor the live Genesis Market through graduation. The future DEX stage must not be described as active before graduation.
7. Complete server/VPS hardening, deployed-bytecode equivalence verification, CI/release hardening and independent external security review readiness.

Mining activation and the Genesis Market are live on BNB Smart Chain. Daily settlement publication and four-hour automatic payout checks are separate processes.
